Correct the single-person 2025–26 annual Medicare thresholds to $28,011 and $35,013 using the enacted amendment. Annual reports now identify rules-au-income-tax/1.0.1 and link the checked legal source. At $30,000 taxable income, the Medicare levy changes from $277.80 to $198.90. Other annual tax components and pay calculators retain their rules.

Accept zero taxable income in the existing nil-rate income-tax band. Positive-income results and nearest-cent arithmetic are unchanged.
