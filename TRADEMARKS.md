# TaxKit trademarks

Cooper Corbett owns the TaxKit name, the Stand mark (the T whose crossbar is a
balance beam) and the TaxKit lockup. The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the code
and documentation in this repository. It grants no rights to these marks. This
policy says how you may use them.

None of the marks is registered. Ownership rests on use.

## Uses that need no permission

- Say that your project uses, integrates with or is built on TaxKit.
- Refer to TaxKit by name in articles, talks, teaching and reviews.
- Install and redistribute unmodified TaxKit packages under their published
  names, such as `@taxkit/sdk`.
- Show the unaltered mark beside a link to TaxKit, for example in a list of
  integrations.

Each of these uses must be truthful and must leave readers able to tell your
work apart from TaxKit.

## Uses that need written permission

- Using TaxKit, the mark or a confusingly similar name or symbol in the name
  or logo of a product, company, package, domain or social account.
- Distributing a modified version of TaxKit under the TaxKit name.
- Suggesting that Cooper Corbett or the TaxKit project endorses, sponsors or
  checked your product or its tax results.
- Altering, recolouring or combining the mark with other marks.
- Putting the mark on merchandise.

## Forks

The MIT licence lets you fork TaxKit freely. A fork you distribute needs its
own name and logo. Keep the copyright notice from [LICENSE](LICENSE), and you
may say that your fork is based on TaxKit.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue at <https://github.com/crcorbett/taxkit/issues> describing the
use you have in mind.
